Tea Leaf & Fried Bean Management System (Shunesein / Ma Cherry)
System Overview — For Client Meeting
=====================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
------------------
The system covers all 23 modules from the original scope, end to end — every
screen a staff member uses is backed by a working process behind it, not a
placeholder. Beyond the original scope, the reporting section now offers far
more reports than originally planned, and the whole system keeps a proper
running set of accounting books in the background, so financial figures
(profit, cash position, outstanding balances) stay accurate automatically as
day-to-day work happens, without anyone needing to do manual bookkeeping.

Every action a user takes — creating a purchase, recording a payment, editing
a product — is logged with who did it and when, and every screen is
permission-controlled, so staff only see and do what their role allows.

Below is a walkthrough of each module: what it does for the business, and
what is available today.


1. DASHBOARD
------------
The homepage after login. At a glance, an owner/manager sees:
- Today's sales, purchases, and production output
- Current cash and bank balances
- How much customers owe the business, and how much the business owes
  suppliers — broken down by how overdue each amount is
- Items running low on stock, ready to reorder
- Best-selling products over the last 30 days
- A 6-month trend chart of sales, expenses, and cash flow
Status: Fully working.


2. USER & PERMISSION MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------
Admins can create staff accounts and assign roles (e.g. Cashier, Store
Manager, Accountant, Owner). Each role can be given fine-grained permissions
— down to individual actions like "can record a payment" or "can view the
Profit & Loss report" — not just broad on/off access per module. The Owner
role is protected and cannot be edited or deleted by mistake.
Status: Fully working.


3. PRODUCT (STOCK) MASTER
---------------------------
The master list of everything the business buys, produces, and sells —
tea leaves, fried beans, raw materials, and finished goods. Each product has
a cost price, a markup rule, and the system automatically calculates the
selling price from it (so pricing stays consistent, even during bulk
updates via spreadsheet import). Supports bulk import/export via CSV.
Status: Fully working.


4. CATEGORY MANAGEMENT
------------------------
Simple grouping of products into categories for organization and reporting.
Status: Fully working.


5. SUPPLIER MANAGEMENT
------------------------
Master list of suppliers with contact details and a running "amount owed to
this supplier" balance that updates automatically from purchases, returns,
and payments — no manual balance entry needed.
Status: Fully working.


6. CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT
------------------------
Master list of customers with contact details, an optional credit limit, and
a running "amount owed by this customer" balance, updated automatically from
sales, returns, and receipts.
Status: Fully working.
Note for discussion: the credit limit field is stored and visible, but the
system does not yet block a new sale if a customer is already over their
limit. This can be added if it's a priority.


7. WAREHOUSE / STORE MANAGEMENT
---------------------------------
Master list of storage locations (main warehouse, branch stores, etc.).
Stock is tracked separately per warehouse, and the system will not allow
stock to go negative at any location.
Status: Fully working.


8. PURCHASE MANAGEMENT
------------------------
Recording goods received from suppliers. When a purchase is recorded, the
system automatically: increases stock at the right warehouse, increases the
amount owed to that supplier, and updates the accounting books. Purchase
returns (sending goods back to a supplier) are also fully supported, with
everything above reversing correctly. Edits are safely restricted once a
payment or a stock movement has already happened against a purchase, to
prevent numbers going out of sync.
Status: Fully working.


9. PRODUCTION ENTRY
---------------------
Records converting raw materials into finished goods (e.g. raw tea leaves
into packaged tea). The system checks that only proper raw materials go in
and only proper finished goods come out, deducts the raw material stock when
production starts, and adds the finished goods to stock once production is
marked complete. Production can also be cancelled midway, with stock
correctly restored.
Status: Fully working.


10. STOCK TRANSFER
--------------------
Moving stock between warehouses/branches, with a clear approval flow:
requested, approved, shipped, received. Stock is only deducted from the
source and added to the destination once the transfer actually completes —
so the stock count always reflects reality, not just a status label.
Status: Fully working.


11. STOCK ADJUSTMENT
----------------------
For correcting stock manually — e.g. damaged, expired, or missing items.
Applied immediately, with a check that stock can never be adjusted below
zero.
Status: Fully working.


12. STOCK COUNT
------------------
Supports a full physical stock count at a warehouse: staff enter what they
physically counted, and the system automatically works out and applies the
difference against what the books say should be there.
Status: Fully working.


13. SALES MANAGEMENT
-----------------------
Recording sales to customers. Mirrors Purchase Management: stock is
automatically reduced, the customer's balance owed increases, and the
accounting books update — all in one step. Sales returns (customer sending
goods back) are fully supported with everything reversing correctly.
Status: Fully working.


14. DELIVERY MANAGEMENT
--------------------------
Tracks getting sold goods to the customer — one delivery trip can cover
multiple invoices at once (useful for customers who order frequently).
Status tracked from pending, to shipped, to delivered.
Status: Fully working.


15. CUSTOMER RECEIPT
-----------------------
Recording money received from a customer against their outstanding
invoices. The system prevents collecting more than what's actually owed,
updates the customer's balance and the relevant cash/bank account, and
supports full reversal if a receipt was entered in error.
Status: Fully working.


16. SUPPLIER PAYMENT
-----------------------
The mirror of Customer Receipt — recording money paid out to a supplier
against what's owed to them, with the same safeguards.
Status: Fully working.


17. CASH & BANK MANAGEMENT
-----------------------------
A full running ledger (like a bank statement) for every cash and bank
account the business uses — every receipt, payment, and transfer shown in
order, with a running balance and an opening balance for any date range you
choose. Also supports moving money directly between the business's own
accounts (e.g. bank to petty cash).
Status: Fully working — this is one of the most complete modules in the
system.


18. INCOME & EXPENSE MANAGEMENT
----------------------------------
Recording other business income and expenses that aren't tied to a purchase
or sale (rent, utilities, misc. income, etc.), categorized and reflected
immediately in the relevant account balance and financial reports.
Status: Fully working.


19. REPORTS
-------------
This module has grown well beyond the original plan. It now includes over
30 reports, covering:
- Stock: current stock, stock ledger (full transaction history), stock
  movement, low stock, stock valuation, slow-moving items, expiry tracking
- Purchasing: purchase summary, purchase order variance, supplier
  performance/scorecards
- Production: daily and monthly production summaries
- Sales: sales summary, salesperson performance, sales by category
- Finance & Accounting: Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, Cash
  Flow Statement, customer/supplier outstanding & aging, tax summary
Every report can be viewed and filtered on screen, and every report's
visibility is controlled per staff role.
Status: Fully working, and the Profit & Loss calculation correctly accounts
for real cost of goods (not just raw purchase totals), giving an accurate
profit figure.


20. AUDIT LOG
---------------
A complete history of who did what and when — every create, edit, delete,
login, and print action across the whole system is recorded, with the
ability to see exactly what changed on any given record. Useful for
accountability and resolving "who changed this" questions.
Status: Fully working.


21. SYSTEM SETTINGS
----------------------
Currently covers print layout settings — configurable page sizes/margins
for printed invoices, receipts, and vouchers, with one profile set as the
default.
Note for discussion: this is the one module scoped narrower than its name
suggests — there's no general "company settings" screen beyond print setup
today. If there are other settings the client expects here (e.g. company
info, tax settings), we should scope that out together.


22. PURCHASE ORDER
----------------------
The planning stage before a Purchase (Module 8) — raising an order to a
supplier, approving it, and converting it into an actual received purchase
once goods arrive. Orders can be edited or cancelled freely before
approval.
Status: Fully working.


23. SALE ORDER
------------------
The planning stage before a Sale (Module 13) — same idea as Purchase Order,
for the sales side: create an order, approve it, then convert it into an
invoice once ready to fulfill.
Status: Fully working.


CLOSING NOTES
--------------
Everything above is live and working end to end — not a demo or a partial
build. On top of the original 23-module scope, the system has grown
significant additional value: 30+ reports instead of the ~9 originally
planned, and full double-entry-style bookkeeping running quietly behind
every transaction so the financial reports can be trusted.
