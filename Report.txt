3. Key findings

- Nothing in Module 19 is reachable by a user. All four existing report endpoints (current-stock, low-stock, sales-summary, profit-loss) plus the dashboard and stock-ledger endpoints are wired with permissions (view_reports, seeded correctly) but have zero frontend consumption — no Reports.jsx, no Dashboard.jsx exist in frontend/src/pages at all, and no fetch call anywhere in the frontend hits any of these routes.
- Profit & Loss is not just missing UI — the calculation itself needs to be redesigned before it's worth exposing.
- 4 of 9 contracted reports have no backend at all (Stock Movement, Purchase Summary, Production Summary, Outstanding/Aging), though all their source data already exists cleanly.
- The Dashboard module (a whole numbered module in the contract) has no page whatsoever — this is likely the single biggest visible gap for the client, since it's the first thing an owner/manager would expect to see after login.
- The one bright spot: Cash & Bank Book (Module 17) is genuinely done — full ledger, already in the UI.

Given this, I'd suggest we sequence the work rather than build all 9 at once — happy to walk through priority with you before touching code.
